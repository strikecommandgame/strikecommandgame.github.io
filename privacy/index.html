Strike Command Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Summary:

  • Strike Command has no accounts, and your game progress stays on your device.
  • The game sends optional crash reports. They contain technical details that help us fix bugs, and you can turn them off in Settings → Send crash reports.
  • Some versions of the game show ads from an advertising partner such as Google AdMob, which collects device data to serve and measure ads. See Advertising for how this works and how to limit it.
  • We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Strike Command ("the game") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, email anwar.alfazaa@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device:

  • mission progress, scores, medals and upgrades;
  • your settings;
  • a small technical log file.

None of this is sent to us, except the log excerpt included in a crash report (see below). Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Crash and error reports

If the game crashes or hits an error, it can send a report so we can fix the problem. Reports are switched on by default, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings → Send crash reports. A report contains:

DataExample
Game version and time of the report0.1.0-demo, 2026-10-16 21:39
Device and system detailsPhone model, Android version, processor, graphics chip and driver version, screen size
Game stateThe screen or mission in use, the selected helicopter and your in-game settings
Technical logError messages and the last lines of the game's log file

A report does not contain your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, advertising ID or any account information, and we do not use it to identify you.

Reports are delivered to our email inbox by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service. Like any internet service, FormSubmit receives your device's IP address while it handles the request, and its own privacy policy applies to that processing. If a report cannot be sent (for example, while you are offline), it waits on your device and is sent the next time you start the game.

We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs. We keep them in our email for as long as they are useful for that purpose, and delete them after 12 months at the latest.

Advertising

To keep the game free, Strike Command may show ads, for example optional "watch an ad for a reward" videos or ads between missions. Ads are delivered by third-party advertising networks, currently planned to be Google AdMob. Test versions of the game without ads collect no data for advertising.

When ads are shown, the ad network collects and processes data directly from your device to serve, personalise, cap and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. This can include:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (not precise GPS location);
  • device and app information, such as the model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped.

The ad network's own privacy policy governs this data. For Google, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's settings.
  • Delete or opt out of your advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads). This switches off personalised ads.
  • Non-personalised ads: if you decline or opt out, you may still see ads, but they are not based on your activity.

We do not receive your advertising ID or personal data from the ad network. We only see combined statistics, such as how many ads were shown.

What we don't do

  • There are no user accounts, sign-in or social features.
  • Apart from the advertising network described above, there are no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your data. We share data only with the service providers described in this policy (crash-report delivery and advertising).
  • The game does not use location, the camera, the microphone, contacts or files outside its own storage.

Permissions

  • Internet: used to send crash reports and, in versions with ads, to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (versions with ads only): used by the ad network as described above.
  • Vibration: used for in-game haptic feedback.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Crash reports contain no personal information. Ads in the game are configured not to target people known to be under the age of consent. If you believe a report includes information about a child, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off reports: switch off crash reports in Settings.
  • Ads: see the choices under Advertising.
  • Delete your data: to have reports from your device deleted, email us with your phone model and the approximate date, and we will remove them.
  • Your local rights: depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA in California), you may have further rights to access or erase data. Email us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting different data, for example if analytics, in-app purchases or a different ad network are added, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
